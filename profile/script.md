# HyperBus: How It Works

A 39.9-second motion piece at 1920×1080, 60 fps.

- **Voice:** Kokoro-82M, voice `af_heart`, at 1.08× speed.
- **Look:** dark navy, neon line work, bloom, and film grain.
- **Palette:** cyan for HyperBus, amber, cyan, and mint for the regions, red for threats.
- **Story:** one shared memory is a single point of failure, and HyperBus turns it into a contained map.

Every visual and sound cue is keyed to the voice's word timestamps, so each beat lands on its word.

---

## 1. One swarm (00:00.0 to 00:03.6)

**Voice:** "One swarm of AI agents. One shared memory."

**Picture:**
- A single spark ignites in the dark.
- 64 agents bloom out of it into a drifting constellation, with lines flickering between neighbors.
- On "shared memory", the swarm wraps into a ring, and every agent wires into one glowing memory orb at the center.
- Captions: `ONE SWARM.` then `ONE SHARED MEMORY.`
- HUD: `SWARM · 64 AGENTS · 1 MEMORY`

**Sound:**
- An ignition swell, a whoosh on "swarm", and a high shimmer on "shared memory".
- A D-minor pad fades in underneath.

## 2. One poisoned prompt (00:03.9 to 00:06.4)

**Voice:** "One poisoned prompt, and it can reach everything."

**Picture:**
- One agent on the ring turns red.
- A comet carries the injection into the orb, and the infection races around the ring until every agent is red.
- The camera lurches, an RGB glitch tears the frame, and the whole system implodes to a single point.
- Captions: `ONE POISONED PROMPT`, then `IT REACHES EVERYTHING.`, shaking.
- HUD: `ALERT · PROMPT INJECTION`

**Sound:**
- Glitch bursts, a zap on "poisoned", and a low thud on "prompt".
- A heartbeat at 112 bpm.
- A riser and a reverse swell pull into the cut.

## 3. HyperBus redraws the map (00:07.0 to 00:08.7)

**Voice:** "HyperBus redraws the map."

**Picture:**
- A white flash and a scan line.
- The HyperBus mark draws itself (hexagon, two pillars, bus line) and the wordmark tracks in.
- The logo flies to the corner while a dot grid ripples out across the new world.
- Caption: `REDRAWS THE MAP.`

**Sound:**
- A sub impact and a bell chime.
- The harmony lifts from D minor to B-flat major.

## 4. Walls and regions (00:09.1 to 00:13.0)

**Voice:** "Every tenant is walled off. Inside, memory is cut into regions."

**Picture:**
- Three tenant chambers (globex, acme, initech) draw their walls.
- Lock pulses travel each perimeter.
- The camera dives into acme. Its memory slab is slashed twice and springs apart into three regions: billing, support, and reporting. Their names decode in.
- Captions: `EVERY TENANT, WALLED OFF.` then `MEMORY, CUT INTO REGIONS.`

**Sound:**
- A wire-draw sweep and lock clicks on "walled off".
- A whoosh into the dive, two metallic slices on "cut", and three pops as the regions land.
- A plucked arpeggio starts.

## 5. The region is in the key (00:13.4 to 00:18.0)

**Voice:** "The region lives in the key itself, so an agent can't even name what it wasn't granted."

**Picture:**
- A storage key assembles chip by chip: `acme / support / thread-42 / ckpt-7`.
- A wire runs from the `support` chip to the support region.
- An address book opens for the agent. `support` gets a check; `billing` and `reporting` are redacted behind locks.
- Captions: `THE REGION IS IN THE KEY.` then `NOT GRANTED? NOT NAMEABLE.`

**Sound:**
- Ticks and clicks as the chips snap in.
- Two marker swipes as the ungranted names are redacted.

## 6. Explicit grants (00:18.4 to 00:22.2)

**Voice:** "Each agent carries explicit grants. Read. Write. Or nothing."

**Picture:**
- Three agents appear under their regions, and grant lines draw up with badges:
  - billing-bot: `READ · WRITE` on billing;
  - support-bot: `READ`, upgrading to `READ · WRITE`, on support;
  - reporting-bot: `READ` on reporting.
- Dashed red lines from support-bot to the other two regions are stamped `⊘ NONE`.
- Caption: `READ. WRITE. OR NOTHING.`, with each word in its grant color.

**Sound:**
- Two rising blips for read and write, and a dull thunk for nothing.

## 7. Hijack (00:22.7 to 00:24.7)

**Voice:** "Hijack one, and it reaches for billing."

**Picture:**
- support-bot glitches red and gets an `INJECTED` tag.
- A lightning beam crackles out of it toward the billing region.
- Captions: `HIJACK ONE…` then `IT REACHES FOR BILLING.`
- HUD: `EVENT · support-bot HIJACKED`

**Sound:**
- A glitch hit and a crackling beam that builds with a riser.
- The heartbeat returns at 140 bpm.
- The harmony drops back to D minor with a flat-nine tension note.

## 8. Denied (00:25.0 to 00:26.9)

**Voice:** "Denied. HyperBus fails closed."

**Picture:**
- The beam slams into a honeycomb shield at the billing gate, throwing shockwave rings and sparks.
- The gate locks, and a `CapabilityError` tag pins to the shield.
- `DENIED.` slams onto the screen in red as the camera kicks.
- Caption: `HYPERBUS FAILS CLOSED.`
- HUD: `EVENT · CapabilityError · DENIED`

**Sound:**
- The biggest impact of the piece, layered with a denial buzzer.
- A gate clank and a lock click on "closed".

## 9. The engine holds the keys (00:27.4 to 00:32.7)

**Voice:** "Agents never hold database credentials. The engine does, and it verifies every caller."

**Picture:**
- The camera cranes down beneath the tenants.
- Credential keys beside the agents are struck out and stream down into the HyperBus engine, a hexagon now holding a single gold key and piped to the database below.
- ID packets travel down each agent's line and get mint checkmarks at the engine.
- An unknown `???` caller is turned away.
- Captions: `AGENTS NEVER HOLD CREDENTIALS.` then `THE ENGINE VERIFIES EVERY CALLER.`
- HUD: `ENGINE · EVERY CALLER VERIFIED`

**Sound:**
- A thunk on "never" and a chime on "credentials".
- An engine hum, two verification blips, and a reject buzz on "caller".

## 10. The blast radius ends at its grants (00:33.2 to 00:37.3)

**Voice:** "HyperBus. When one agent falls, the blast radius ends at its grants."

**Picture:**
- The camera pulls back to all three tenants.
- support-bot falls. A red blast wave expands, then stops dead at a hull drawn around exactly what it was granted, the support region.
- Outside the hull, a calm mint ripple shows everything else untouched.
- Label: `BLAST RADIUS = ITS GRANTS`
- Captions: `WHEN ONE AGENT FALLS,` then `THE BLAST RADIUS ENDS AT ITS GRANTS.`
- HUD: `CONTAINED · BLAST RADIUS = GRANTS`

**Sound:**
- A whoosh into the pull-back, a heavy thud on "falls", and a shimmer on "ends".
- The harmony resolves to D major.

## 11. End card (00:37.4 to 00:39.9)

**Picture:**
- The world dims behind the HyperBus logo, which returns to center with a light sweep.
- Tagline: **Containment for AI agent swarms.**
- Sub-line: `TENANT · REGION · GRANT · FAIL CLOSED`

**Sound:**
- A final impact and chime over the full-chord swell, fading out.
